# Historical baseline — 2026-08-30

## Verdict

**The fixed BTC/USD and ETH/USD desk has no demonstrated tradable edge.**

- Zero of eight 90/180/365/730-day tests produced a positive net return.
- All eight profit factors were below 1.0 and all realized expectancies were negative.
- Positive excess return in the 365-day decline means the desk lost less than buy-and-hold; it did not make money.
- The signal failed in rising and falling markets. Live capital remains **$0**.

This is the corrected canonical baseline. It includes causal indicator warm-up before each exact evaluation boundary and makes the historical drawdown halt liquidate at the next observable bar open, matching the fail-closed paper executor. No signal, threshold, agent weight, fee, slippage, or sizing parameter was changed.

## Fixed specification

- Source: Alpaca hourly crypto bars
- Cutoff: 2026-08-30 16:00 UTC; forming bar excluded
- Starting equity: $10,000 per symbol/window
- Signal on completed bar T; execution at T+1 open
- Long-only, no leverage
- Costs: 10 bps fee + 5 bps slippage each way
- Gap-through and entry-bar stops modeled
- Risk: 0.35% per trade, 20% symbol cap, 35% total cap, 1% daily halt, 8% persistent drawdown halt
- Each symbol/window is an independent replay, not a combined portfolio
- Thirty days of pre-period data provide causal warm-up; performance starts exactly at the requested boundary

## Why it failed by regime

### Rising markets: poor participation and poor selection

Buy-and-hold gained 11–26% in the 90-day windows and 16–26% in the 180-day windows. The strategy participated only 15–29% of the time yet still lost money. This is not merely a conservative beta tradeoff: win rates fell as low as 18%, and every expectancy remained negative. The EMA/momentum desk repeatedly bought short-lived strength and failed to retain enough of persistent advances.

### Falling markets: capital preservation, not alpha

During the 365-day windows, BTC and ETH buy-and-hold lost 27.66% and 43.29%. The desk lost about 8%, creating positive excess returns only because exposure was low and the drawdown halt stopped additional risk. An absolute-return strategy that loses money has not established an edge merely because the benchmark lost more.

### Trading friction amplified—but did not create—the failure

Adding modeled costs back to net P&L gives a rough pre-cost diagnostic:

| Symbol | 90d | 180d | 365d | 730d |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| BTC/USD | +$146.79 | -$267.14 | -$254.25 | +$553.47 |
| ETH/USD | +$58.79 | -$372.99 | -$234.54 | -$63.06 |

Six of eight samples were negative even before this simple cost add-back. In the two positive raw samples, turnover consumed the advantage. Average holds of 9–12 hours are too short for a weak hourly signal under 30 bps modeled round-trip friction.

### The drawdown control dominated longer paths

The 8% portfolio rule persistently halted the affected replay and liquidated any open position at the next bar open. That is correct capital protection, but it also means long-window inactivity is evidence of strategy failure—not evidence that the signal later recovered.

## Audit corrections

The baseline implementation was corrected only for simulation integrity:

1. Entry-bar lows can trigger a newly opened position's stop.
2. Forming candles are excluded at a fixed UTC cutoff.
3. Pre-period data warm indicators without shortening the requested evaluation interval.
4. Portfolio/daily halts liquidate at the next observable open and persistent drawdown halts remain closed.

The historical result remains unambiguous after these corrections: no tested window has positive net expectancy.

## Limitations

- Overlapping windows are not independent trials.
- Tests are per-symbol; the portfolio-level 35% cap is not fully exercised.
- Hourly OHLC bars do not reveal intrabar path.
- Flat costs do not model venue-, size-, spread-, or volatility-dependent execution.
- The fixed signal predates this report, but no untouched forward sample has established generalization.
- No news, order-book, funding, macro, sentiment, or on-chain inputs are included.

Do not tune thresholds against these windows. Use this result as the frozen control for causal, chronological candidate tests.
